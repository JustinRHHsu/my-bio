- **Agentic Company** — AI systems that run a company's operations end to end and keep learning on their own, built on loop engineering and data flywheels.
- **On-premise models** — private AI that runs on a company's own hardware, so its data never leaves the building.
- **Humane AI products** — digital products with a human and cultural heart, made for everyday life.

Earlier experiments: [Photo Studio](https://dub.sh/photo-studio), an AI photo studio, and [Marco Polo](https://www.instagram.com/marcopolo.tourguide/), an AI travel guide I built during 90 days of travelling in Europe.
